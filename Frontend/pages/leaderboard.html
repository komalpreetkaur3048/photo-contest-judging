    FAIR JUDGING

    Built to reduce judging bias.

    Different judges may naturally score differently. One judge may be stricter, while another may give higher scores.

    PhotoJudge normalizes judge scores before calculating the final ranking, helping reduce the influence of individual judging styles.

    01

    Collect

    Scores are collected independently from each judge.

    02

    Normalize

    Judge scoring differences are adjusted before aggregation.

    03

    Rank

    Normalized scores are combined to determine the final ranking.
